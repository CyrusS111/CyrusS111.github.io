What we do

Find where your power is going and stop paying for waste.

Retrofitting Legacy Machines

Give old, reliable machines modern controls instead of replacing them.

  • Lower running costs, longer machine life

Industrial Automation

Reliable, repeatable processes with less manual work.

Smart Controls

  • Automatic switching and timers
  • Remote monitoring and alerts

Electrical Installations

  • Inspections, fault-finding and upgrades

About us

A Ugandan engineering company built on practical experience.

EL-SYS Controls & Engineering Ltd was incorporated in Uganda in 2022 to bring dependable automation, energy and electrical engineering to local businesses, factories, institutions and homes.

The company is led by Joseph Bwamiki, an electrical systems engineer with over ten years' experience in RF, electro-mechanical and power systems up to 33 kV, as well as PLC programming, CAD design and technical training. He holds a BEng in Electrical & Electronics Engineering and a Diploma in Mechanical Engineering.

Our belief is simple: Uganda's next energy and industrial gains will come from smarter use of what we already have, and from making more of what we need right here.
