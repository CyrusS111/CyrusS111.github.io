Common questions

CNC and fabrication questions

Can a broken machine part be made in Uganda instead of imported?

Often, yes. Gears, shafts, bushes, brackets, guards and many other parts can be machined or fabricated locally from a drawing or the broken sample, usually faster than an import order.

What materials can you machine?

Mild and stainless steel, aluminium, brass and engineering plastics. Tell us how the part is used and we'll recommend the material.

Do you make one-off parts?

Yes. One-off spares and small batches are a large part of our work, as well as repeat parts for factories.

How is the price worked out?

By material, machining time and finishing. You get a fixed price and a delivery date before anything is made.

Will you keep my drawings confidential?

Yes. Your files are shared only with the workshop making your job, and we sign an NDA on request.
