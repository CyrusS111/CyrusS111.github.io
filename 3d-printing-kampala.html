Common questions

3D printing questions

How much does 3D printing cost in Kampala?

It depends on the material, the size of the part and the print time. You get a fixed price before anything is printed. Send the file or photos on WhatsApp for a quote.

How long does a 3D print take?

Most prints are ready within days. Large parts, resin prints and batches take longer; the quote states the delivery date.

I don't have a 3D file. Can you still print the part?

Yes. Send clear photos of the part with a ruler beside it, or the broken sample. We measure it and model it in 3D first.

Which material is strongest?

For most functional parts PETG or nylon are the best choice; ABS or ASA suit heat and sunlight, and TPU suits parts that must flex. We recommend a material when you tell us how the part is used.

Can a 3D printed part replace a metal part?

Sometimes, for low loads. For gears, shafts and parts under heavy load we usually recommend CNC machining in metal instead.
