Common questions

Parts, fabrication and 3D printing: your questions answered

Can you make a part in Uganda instead of importing it?

Often, yes. We arrange CNC machining, laser and plasma cutting, sheet metal and welding, gears and shafts, 3D printing, PCB assembly and control panel building in verified local workshops, and check every part before delivery.

I don't have a drawing. Can you still make the part?

Yes. Send a photo of the part with a ruler beside it, or the broken sample. We measure it, model it in 3D, produce drawings and quote the job. The design files are yours on payment.

Which files do you accept?

STEP, STL, IGES, SLDPRT, F3D, DXF, DWG, PDF drawings, Gerber or KiCad for electronics, or simply photos with a ruler beside the part.

How fast can you 3D print a part?

Usually within days. Prints are priced by material, size and print time and quoted before printing. Materials include PLA, PETG, ABS/ASA, TPU, nylon and resin.

Are the branded parts genuine?

Yes. Branded items such as SMC, Festo, Schneider Electric, Omron, Mean Well and SKF are genuine stock from certified suppliers or their authorised representatives.

How long does delivery take?

Parts marked in stock are available in Kampala. Parts from local suppliers take 2 to 5 days, and import orders 2 to 4 weeks. We deliver in Kampala or send by courier upcountry.

How do I order and pay?

Add parts to your order list on this page and send it on WhatsApp, or send your drawing for a fixed quote. Prices are in UGX including VAT and are confirmed on your quote. Pay by mobile money or bank transfer.

Are my drawings kept confidential?

Yes. Your files are shared only with the workshop making your job, and we sign an NDA on request.
