3D printing service

Print it in days: prototypes, housings, jigs and spares

Send an STL, STEP or 3MF file, or a photo and sizes and we'll model it. We print, check the fit and deliver. Ideal for one-offs, short runs and parts that are hard to import.

Materials

PLA: prototypes, modelsPETG: tough, outdoorABS / ASA: heat, UV TPU: flexible, gasketsNylon: wear partsResin: fine detail

Good for

  • Product prototypes and enclosures
  • Jigs, fixtures and drill guides
  • Knobs, clips, covers, brackets
  • Replacement plastic parts

What to send

  • File: .stl .step .3mf .obj
  • Material and colour
  • Quantity and needed-by date
  • No file? A photo with a ruler

Pricing

Priced by material, size and print time, quoted before printing. Design or modelling help is quoted separately.
